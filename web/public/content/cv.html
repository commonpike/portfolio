I've studied Mathematics in Leiden, NL and Photography/Audio-Visual Design in Rotterdam, NL, which I finished in 1995. At the moment I am self-employed within the partnership "kennis Werkt", Rotterdam, NL.

During the last 30 years I have been professionally involved in various projects involving internet, culture and data exchange, usually as a freelancer, often part of a team. Although educated as a designer (at the art academy in Rotterdam), my IT background was higher valued and I accumulated the practical knowledge and skills as time went by. Nowadays I am mostly known for a broad practical experience and wide applicability, and the capacity to learn quickly and adapt.

In the remaining time, if any, I am a graphic artist, musician and sound designer.
